## What ALVR does

ALVR streams PC VR games to supported headsets over the local network.
This repository contains ALVR software; the [alvr-visionos client](https://github.com/cbusillo/alvr-visionos) holds the Apple Vision Pro app.
Evidence: [README.md](README.md) and the linked client repository.
